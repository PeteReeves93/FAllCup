The GNASHBBL Seven

In 2072 a crack Blood Bowl team was sent off for a foul they didn't commit. These players promptly escaped a maximum-security jail to the Middenheim underground. Today, still wanted by the N.A.F., they survive as mercenaries of fortune. If you have a problem, and no one else can help… maybe you can hire one of the GNASHBBL Seven.

GNASH Mercs Up to two GNASH Mercs may be induced (all tiers).

Notes

Stats are MA / ST / AG / PA / AV. Every merc has Loner (4+). "Induce" is the gold cost to hire the merc for a single match as an inducement.
