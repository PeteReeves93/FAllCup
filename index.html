How it works

Coaches each get 1,150,000 gp and a pot of starting SPP set by their team's tier to build a fresh squad. Depending on numbers there may be preliminary or group stages to reach the last 16 or 8. Teams knocked out get a shot at the prestigious F.All Plate in Dungeon Bowl. The final is aimed at the first week of December.
